=== FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000) ===
Astro Key reads your chart to you. Every placement comes with its own written explanation in everyday language, and every position is calculated with Swiss Ephemeris, the engine behind professional astrology software. No account and no ads: your charts stay on your phone.

★ YOUR CHART, IN WORDS
• Who you are: your Sun, Moon and Ascendant
• How each planet shows up: every body by house and sign
• How it plays out: the house a planet stands in, the ruler of that house, and where that ruler is. This is the reading that belongs to your chart and no one else's
• Over 10,000 written readings. Each combination has its own text, never stitched together from fragments
• Two ways in: plain language first, or the chart first for astrologers. Switch at any time

★ NATAL CHART
• Chart wheel with aspects, planet table with dignities, house cusps and an aspect grid
• Sun to Pluto, Chiron, the North Node and Lilith, plus the Ascendant and Midheaven
• House systems: Placidus, Whole Sign, Koch, Equal, Regiomontanus, Porphyry
• Import the charts you have saved on Astro.com

★ TRANSITS
• Today's transits to your natal chart, strongest first, each explained in plain words
• See when a transit enters orb, becomes exact and leaves, including the extra passes a planet makes when it turns retrograde
• A strength curve shows every stretch of time the aspect is active
• Move through time by the hour, day, week, month or year

★ SYNASTRY
One resonance score for two people, with a written card for every place where your charts answer each other, and for the places where they don't. The cards talk about your life together, not about planets.

★ HUMAN DESIGN
• Type, strategy, authority, profile, definition, centres and channels
• Open centres: where you stop being yourself
• Hanging gates: what you look for in other people
• Today's gate transits against your own chart
• The four arrows: determination, environment, motivation and perspective
• Raising a child according to their type and authority
• Connection chart for two people

★ PRIMARY DIRECTIONS
Placidus semi-arc directions with a True Solar Equatorial Arc key, shown on a biwheel.

★ EVENT DIARY
Record the moments that matter. Each event gets its own chart, a photo if you like, tags, and a link to the person it belongs to. Every event shows its tightest aspect as a sentence, so the diary reads without knowing the glyphs.

★ NOW
The sky at this moment, also as a home-screen widget.

No sign-up, no account, no ads. Everything is stored on your device and works offline. The internet is used only to look up a city.
